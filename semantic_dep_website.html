Welcome

Learn. Build. Innovate.

The Department of Computer Science was started in 2005 and offers both undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. We run six labs, an AI and cyber security research cell, and an industry interaction wing. The department has 18 full time faculty members and around 600 students.

About the Department

Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computer science education and research, and to produce professionals who are technically strong and socially responsible.

Mission

  • Teach a syllabus that keeps up with current industry practice.
  • Encourage research, innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Build industry tie ups through internships and live projects.
  • Promote ethics, teamwork and lifelong learning.

Objectives

  1. Build problem solving skills in algorithms, systems and software design.
  2. Give practical exposure through lab work, mini projects and internships.
  3. Improve communication and leadership along with technical skills.
  4. Support students who go for certifications or higher studies.
  5. Give back to the community through technical outreach programmes.

Courses

BCS (Bachelor of Computer Science)

Three year full time programme with six semesters and 140 credits. Every semester has four theory papers and one lab course.

Semester Wise Subjects

Semester Subjects Lab
Semester 1 Programming in C, Digital Fundamentals, Discrete Mathematics, Communicative English C Programming Lab
Semester 2 C++ and OOPs, Data Structures, Computer Organisation, Statistics Data Structures Lab
Semester 3 Java Programming, DBMS, Operating Systems, Front End Frameworks Java and DBMS Lab
Semester 4 Computer Networks, Software Engineering, Python, Analysis of Algorithms Networks and Python Lab
Semester 5 Artificial Intelligence, Web Technologies, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security Web Technologies Lab
Semester 6 Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, Elective (Mobile / IoT / DevOps), Project Work Major Project and Viva
