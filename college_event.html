About the Event

TechNova is the annual technical symposium of the Department of Computer Science. Every October it brings students from colleges across Karnataka together for two days of talks, workshops and competitions.

The theme for 2026 is Building Responsible AI. Speakers from industry and research will talk about building software that people can trust, and the hackathon problem statements come from real needs of local communities.

Whether you want to compete, learn a new tool or just see what other students are building, there is something here for you.

Event Highlights

Six ways to take part over the two days.

01

24-Hour Hackathon

Teams of two to four build a working prototype on problem statements announced at kick-off. Mentors stay on the floor through the night.

02

Hands-on Workshops

Three guided workshops on web development, cloud deployment and machine learning. Bring your own laptop.

03

Industry Keynotes

Engineers and researchers talk about what they are building and what they look for when they hire freshers.

04

Paper Presentation

Present your research to a panel of faculty judges. The best papers are recommended to the college journal.

05

Project Expo

Show your mini or major project to visitors, faculty and recruiters. Stalls are given on a first come basis.

06

Prizes and Certificates

A prize pool of ₹1,50,000 across all events, and a participation certificate for every registered student.
